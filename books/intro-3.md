# What it means to be a developer now

If the value is moving away from just writing code, what's left? What actually matters now?

Based on my own experience, the answer comes down to three things:

- **Systemic thinking** — the ability to see how pieces fit together, not just produce one piece on request
- **Using AI as a tool, not a replacement** — knowing when to reach for it and when not to
- **Turning AI's capabilities into systems** — building things that solve real business problems, not just generating snippets of code

**The role has shifted from "writes code" to "builds solutions."**

Nobody needs a developer who just types out what's already been specified — a model does that faster now. What's needed instead is someone who can look at a business problem, figure out where AI genuinely helps, and assemble that into something that actually works for the company using it.

That's the developer this book is written for — and the one I'm trying to become.
