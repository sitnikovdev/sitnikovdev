## 5.4 Three Projections of One Token: Q, K, V

Every token already has an embedding — a vector of numbers encoding its meaning (Chapter 4). To let tokens "address" each other, that embedding gets passed through **three separate trainable matrices** — W_Q, W_K, and W_V — producing three smaller vectors:

- **Q** — "what I'm looking for"
- **K** — "what I have to offer, if someone comes looking for me"
- **V** — "what I'll actually hand over, if I get picked"

Importantly, these aren't three different tokens or three roles assigned to different words — **they're the same token, viewed from three different angles.**

And just like the embeddings themselves, nobody hand-tunes these three matrices — the model learns them during training, simply because that configuration helps it predict the next token more accurately.
