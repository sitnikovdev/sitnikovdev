## 5.2 The Turning Point: Dropping Sequence

In 2017, a paper called **"Attention Is All You Need"** proposed a radical shift: what if we abandoned the idea of reading text in order altogether?

Instead of processing tokens one after another, passing "memory" along step by step, the authors proposed letting **every token reach directly to any other token** in the sentence — no matter how far apart they stood.

> Token #1 could "see" token #50 just as easily as token #2 — no chain of intermediate steps through which information had to "seep."

This was the attention mechanism: not a gradual passing of memory, but a **direct, simultaneous comparison** of every token against every other.
