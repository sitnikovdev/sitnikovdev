## 5.6 A Token's New Representation: A Weighted Sum of V

Once a token has its set of weights — one per token in the sentence, summing to one — those weights are used to **blend the V-vectors of all the tokens into one new vector.**

The token, which until now stood on its own, becomes a mixture of information from the whole sentence — not in equal parts, but in exactly the proportion the weights from the previous step determined.

- A token paying close "attention" to a neighbor **absorbs more** from it
- One that barely attended to another **takes almost nothing** from it

This is the moment a token stops being an isolated word and becomes **part of the shared context.**
