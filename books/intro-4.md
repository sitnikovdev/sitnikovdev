# Why this book exists and who it's for

This book exists because I needed it and couldn't find it written the way I wanted to read it.

Most material on LLMs falls into one of two camps: deeply academic explanations aimed at researchers, or surface-level hype pieces that skip the "how" entirely. **What was missing was something written from the perspective of a working developer** — someone learning this the way developers actually learn: by building, breaking things, and writing down what finally clicked.

That's what this book is. Not a course, not a textbook — **notes from my own path** into AI Engineering, starting from an iOS development background, working through the material one real experiment at a time.

**This book is for you if:**

- You're a developer, not a data scientist, and the math-heavy explanations lose you before the point lands
- You want to understand what's actually happening under the hood, not just call an API and hope for the best
- You'd rather build something small and real than read a hundred pages of theory first
- You're figuring out, like I am, what your role looks like now that AI can write code too

I'm not writing this as someone who has already arrived. I'm writing it _while_ making the transition — which means every chapter reflects something I genuinely had to work through, not something I already knew and am now explaining from a distance.
